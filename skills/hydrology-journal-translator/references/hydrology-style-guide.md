# Hydrology Journal Style Guide

## Core rules
- Translate scientific meaning and rhetorical function together.
- Preserve all numerical values, units, equations, model names, scenarios, and evidence scope.
- Results emphasize evidence; Discussion explains mechanisms.

## Results pattern
Use: indicator -> value/change -> spatial or temporal difference -> synthesis.
Prefer:
- Streamflow increased/decreased...
- SPEI-12 exhibited...
- SWAT reproduced...

Avoid repeated literal structures such as 'The results showed that'.

## Discussion pattern
Use: finding -> hydrological mechanism -> comparison -> implication -> boundary.
Do not convert correlation, lag analysis, feature importance, or scenario decomposition into causal proof.

## Hydrology terminology
Keep distinctions between runoff and streamflow, groundwater storage and level, soil moisture and storage, drought and water stress, calibration and validation, projection and prediction.

## SWAT terminology
Use HRU, subbasin, reach, surface runoff, lateral flow, groundwater contribution, percolation, evapotranspiration, potential evapotranspiration, soil water according to the actual model variable.

## Evidence verbs
Use reproduced/captured/simulated for model results; associated with/correlated with for statistics; suggests/is consistent with for mechanisms; scenario-based contribution language for decomposition studies.
